New to terrariums?

Everything you need to know

Watering

Closed terrariums need almost no watering — once a month at most. If condensation appears on the glass, you're in balance. Open terrariums every 2 weeks.

Light

Bright, indirect light is ideal. Never place in direct sun — it overheats the glass. A spot near a window facing away from direct sun is perfect. Artificial light works too.

Temperature

Room temperature is fine. Avoid extreme heat, cold drafts, or positioning near AC vents. Most plants in our terrariums thrive between 16–26°C.

Pruning

Trim any yellowing leaves or plants that outgrow the glass. This keeps the ecosystem in balance. You'll receive a care card with your order with exact instructions.
