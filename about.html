Our Beginning

Born from a love of wild places.

Terrafinity started as a quiet obsession — a small glass jar, a handful of moss, and a question: what if you could carry the forest with you? What began as a hobby in a Manila apartment grew into a studio, and then a community of people who share the same need for stillness and nature.

Every design draws from the geography of the Philippine archipelago. The misty ridges of the Cordillera, the tidal pools of El Nido, the mangrove roots of Sulu — these are not just names on our vessels. They are the landscapes that shape each composition, each choice of plant, each pour of substrate.

We make terrariums by hand, to order, one at a time. No mass production. No shortcuts. Just quiet, careful work — the same way nature works.

What we stand for

Principles we build by.

Three beliefs guide every vessel we make, every plant we choose, and every box we ship.

Rooted in place

Every piece begins with a real location — an island, a mountain range, a body of water. We research the native flora, the light conditions, the micro-climate. That specificity is what makes each design honest.

Craft over convenience

We do not mass-produce. Every terrarium is assembled by hand — layered substrate, placed stones, positioned plants. It takes time. That time is visible in the result, and it is what makes your piece one-of-a-kind.

Long-term thinking

A terrarium is a closed or semi-closed ecosystem. When done right, it sustains itself for years. We choose plants that belong together, materials that do not off-gas, and designs that age gracefully rather than trend quickly.

The process

From concept to living art.

Each terrarium passes through four stages before it reaches your hands.

01

Concept & location

We choose a Philippine place as the anchor. Its ecology, color palette, and atmosphere guide every design decision that follows.

02

Material selection

Activated charcoal, drainage grit, sphagnum moss, living substrate — each layer serves a biological function. We never compromise the ecosystem for aesthetics.

03

Hand assembly

Layer by layer, plant by plant. We work slowly, adjusting the composition until the scene feels genuinely natural — not staged or symmetrical.

04

Care & dispatch

Each piece is cured for 48–72 hours to confirm the ecosystem is stable, then packed with custom foam inserts and shipped nationwide across the Philippines.

The inspiration

Named after the islands we love.

Every product in the Terrafinity series carries the name of a Philippine place. Not as branding — as tribute. The Sulu terrarium uses plants that grow near tidal edges. El Nido evokes the limestone karsts of Palawan with its mineral-rich substrate and sparse, dramatic arrangement.

The archipelago has over 7,600 islands. We have only just begun.
